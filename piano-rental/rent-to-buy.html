Rent to buy a piano in Melbourne

Take a Yamaha upright, hand-picked in Japan and checked in Melbourne, home now and pay for it month by month. Every dollar counts toward a price fixed the day you sign. No fee, no interest, and you can stop after 3 months.

How it adds up

The monthly payment is the price divided by 24, rounded up to the next $5. The $200 delivery fee counts toward the price too, so most pianos are paid off a little sooner.

The promises in writing

  • Every dollar counts. All your rent and the delivery fee go toward the price.
  • The price cannot go up. It is fixed the day you sign.
  • You never pay more than the price. There is no fee, no late fee and no interest.
  • You can pay it off at any time. Your own page always shows what is left.
  • You can stop. After the first 3 months, give us 14 days' notice and we collect it at no charge.
  • It is looked after. We tune it once a year in your home and fix faults from normal use at our cost.

Rent-to-buy, or a rental?

A rental costs less each month, but the piano goes back at the end and you have no right to buy it. Rent-to-buy costs more each month because every dollar is working toward owning the piano. If you are fairly sure you will keep playing, it is the better fit. If you only need a piano for a while, a rental is.

Rent-to-buy questions

Is rent-to-buy a loan?

You hire the piano and hold a right to buy it at the price in your agreement. We charge no fee and no interest, and you can never pay more than that price.

What happens to my payments if I give it back?

What you paid covered your time with the piano. It is not refunded, and it no longer counts toward any piano. We collect at no charge and refund your bond, in full unless rent is owing or there is damage beyond fair wear and tear.

Can I pay it off early?

Yes, at any time. Your own page shows what is left, which is simply the price less everything you have paid toward it.

When does the piano become mine?

On the day what you have paid toward it reaches the price. The 10-year warranty starts that day.

Why do you need my date of birth?

Until the piano is yours we record our interest in it on the Personal Property Securities Register, a national register, and that record needs your date of birth. We remove it when you own the piano.

Do you run a credit check?

No. You need to be over 18, live in metro Melbourne and show photo ID to our mover at delivery. We check it and keep no copy.
