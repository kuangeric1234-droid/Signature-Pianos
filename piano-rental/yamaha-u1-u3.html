Rent a Yamaha U1 or U3 in Melbourne

The U1 and the U3 are the uprights most people ask for, and the two heights you will meet in every Yamaha below. Here is how the models we rent compare.

Choose a piano

121 cm or 131 cm

Height is the first choice. A taller upright has longer strings, which gives a fuller bass and a bigger sound. A 121 cm piano sits more easily under a window and suits a smaller room. A 131 cm piano suits a player who wants the sound to fill the room. Both weigh well over 200 kg, which is why only our mover moves them.

Yamaha questions

Is a Yamaha U1 or U3 better for a beginner?

Either. The U1 is 121 cm tall and the U3 is 131 cm. The taller piano has longer strings and a fuller bass, while the U1 fits more easily under a window or in a smaller room. Both are full 88-note acoustic pianos.

How old are your rental pianos?

Most were made between 1979 and 1995, with some from 2002 to 2023. You can check a Yamaha's age yourself with our serial number lookup.

Can I try one first?

Yes. Book a visit to our Mount Waverley showroom and play them.

How much does it cost to rent a piano in Melbourne?

Our Yamaha uprights rent from $115 to $190 a month, GST included. Delivery is $200, collection is $200 and a $500 bond comes back at the end. A Yamaha U1A, 1985 at $125 a month comes to $1,150 over 6 months, plus the bond.
