# Yamaha U / UX / Y uprights, 1970–1999: research notes

Web research, 19 September 2026, for the Signature Pianos Yamaha buyer's guide.

Reliability: production months are from Yamaha Japan's own model list [1] (strongest). Serial ranges are from
Yamaha Piano Service, a Yamaha subsidiary [2][3], described by Yamaha as approximate, shown in millions.
Dimensions and weights come from Japanese dealers, since Yamaha publishes no spec sheets for discontinued models;
"n src" = n dealers agree, "1 src" = indicative only. piano1.jp [8] contains known errors (U3A as 127 cm, wrong
U30BL dates, 1980s YUS mixed with the modern YUS). Pedals: S soft, M muffler, D damper, Sost sostenuto.

## Corrections to the old Classic Pianos guide

- U1A ended 1987.03; U10Bl 1987.04–1989.09; U10A 1989.10–1994.03; U100 1994.03–1997.08. No 1992–94 gap.
- U3A 1982.12–1987.02; U30Bl 1987.04–1989.09; U30A 1989.10–1994.03; U300 1994.03–1997.08.
- UX1 to 1988.03; UX10Bl 1988.04–1990.03; UX10A 1990.04–1994.03; UX100 from 1994.03.
- UX from 1975.05 (not 1974); UX5 to 1988.03; UX50Bl 1988.04–1990.03; UX50A from 1990.04.
- Old U1/U3 dimensions were the current models' (1530 wide, 228/246 kg). Vintage: U1 line ~1210×1500×610, 210–218 kg;
  U3H/M/A 1310×1540×650, 240–248 kg; U30 series 1310×1530×640–650, 235–241 kg; UX1 1210×1500×610–620, 219 kg;
  UX3 1310×1540×650, 238–242 kg; UX5 1310×1540×650, 249 kg; UX50A/UX500 1560×660.
- "Rice-shaped X-brace" is not a Yamaha term (echo of Chinese 米字); use "X-type back post".
- UX500 sostenuto: supported (muffler on a hand lever). YUA sostenuto: disputed. UX5 agraffes: tenor and bass.
- Missing: UX2, the U2 line, U1F/U1G/U3F/U3G, factory silent versions.

## General notes

- Model code and serial are stamped on the iron frame, upper right, visible with the lid open [2][5]. Yamaha
  Japan's approximate first serial of each year (uprights and grands combined) [4]: 1972 1.34M, 1974 1.74M,
  1976 2.15M, 1978 2.57M, 1980 3.03M, 1982 3.48M, 1984 3.89M, 1986 4.21M, 1988 4.56M, 1990 4.82M, 1992 5.08M,
  1994 5.30M, 1996 5.46M, 1997 5.52M, 1998 5.59M, 1999 5.81M. Quote years as "c.".
- Letters mean generation, not grade. "A" was used twice (1950s and 1982–87). Bl black, Wn walnut, Tk teak,
  BiC/MhC birch/mahogany with Chippendale-style legs [2].
- Grey market: Yamaha says it seasons wood per market, and offers no factory warranty or parts support outside
  the original market (US, 2017) [23]. Piano Buyer calls the warnings exaggerated; advises mid-1980s or later in
  good condition, a warranty of at least 5 years, humidity control where very dry [24]. UK importer reports no
  humidity comebacks (commercial interest) [25].
- Yamaha advises avoiding sudden temperature changes, keeping heater air off the piano, and using plants or a
  humidifier against dryness [27].
- Yamaha Piano Service's used-piano checklist: soundboard cracks or warping, deep hammer grooves, rusty strings,
  tuning and voicing history, test play [26]. UK reconditioner's standard list: tapes, loop cords, butt springs,
  checking leathers, key bushings, hammer and damper felts, centres [21]. Japanese dealers commonly restring the
  bass on 1978–83 X models [14][12].
- Sostenuto models (UX5, UX50Bl, UX50A, UX500, probably YUA) have a hand-lever muffler instead of a muffler pedal.
- Yamaha's own refurbished line in Japan: "Renewal Piano" [26].
