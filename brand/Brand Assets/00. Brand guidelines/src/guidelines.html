Signature Pianos · Brand guidelines
01 · The brand
Hand-picked in Japanchecked in Melbourne
Signature Pianos sells new and pre-loved Japanese acoustic pianos from a showroom in Mount Waverley. Every piano is chosen in Japan, imported direct and checked in our Melbourne workshop before it goes on the floor. These pages keep everything we make, on screen and on paper, looking like it came from the same room.
What we sell

Japanese pianos, imported direct

New and pre-loved Yamaha and Kawai uprights and grands, with the occasional European piano that comes through the same buyers. Each one is photographed in Japan before import.

Digital pianos come later. They stay out of the brand's front door until they are in stock.

Who it is for

People buying their first real piano

Parents choosing for a child who has started lessons, usually on a phone, often at night. Adults coming back to the piano after years away. Serious students who know the model they want. People giving a piano as a gift.

None of them should need to know the jargon.

How it should feel

Light, quiet, unhurried

Like a concert hall in daylight before anyone arrives: pale walls, one instrument, room to look at it. The pianos do the talking and the page stays out of their way.

Not the black-and-gold piano showroom. Not a music shop. Not an agency showreel.

The one rule

Lead with Japan

Whatever a page, a letter or a post says first, the fact that each piano is chosen in Japan and checked in Melbourne is never more than a line away. It is the reason to buy from us.

When a question is not answered in these pages: lighter, quieter, fewer.

04 · Logo misuse
Eight waysto spoil it
Each of these has happened to a piano dealer's logo somewhere. None of them happens to ours.
Signature
PIANOS

1  Retyped in another script or sans. Always use the master files.

2  Recoloured, gold above all. The logo is Ink, Key Ivory, White or Black.

3  Stretched or squashed. Scale it by its corner only.

PIANOS

4  PIANOS moved, enlarged or reset. It sits centred under the script at 10 : 58.

5  Given a shadow, glow, outline, bevel or gradient.

6  Placed on a busy photograph with no scrim behind it.

7  Set in a pale colour that cannot be read. Mist on Key Ivory is 1.4 : 1.

8  Rotated, set on a curve or animated letter by letter.

09 · Photography
The real pianoin good light
Four kinds of picture. The most important is the plainest: the actual piano for sale, photographed in Japan before import, exactly as it is. People trust a real photo of a real piano more than a beautiful one of a different piano.

The piano itself

Straight on, whole instrument, lid closed, keys visible. Shot where it stands. The serial-numbered piano we are selling, never a stand-in.

The craft

Action, hammers, strings, pedals, close and sharp. Warm, natural light. Shows what the workshop checks.

The hall

One piano in a large, calm room. For heroes and section openers only, and only with a confirmed licence.

The hands

Someone playing, a lesson, a first try in the showroom. Real customers and teachers, with their permission.

NeverStock families smiling at a keyboard · a piano shown as ours that is not in stock · gold light leaks, lens flare, heavy filters · the black studio backdrop of the old site · AI-generated pianos presented as real · watermarks, phone-flash glare, clutter left in frame

10 · Voice
Plain wordsreal facts
We sound like a good piano technician explaining things to a parent: patient, exact, never selling. Australian English. Numbers as numerals. "Pre-loved", not "used" or "second-hand".
RuleSayNot
1 · Lead with Japan"Hand-picked in Japan, checked in our Mount Waverley workshop.""Melbourne's best piano store."
2 · Name the piano"Yamaha YUS5, 2008, walnut. In the showroom now.""Stunning premium upright in amazing condition!"
3 · Explain once, simply"A silent system lets you play with headphones, so the house stays quiet.""Features SH2 silent technology with CFX binaural sampling."
4 · Reassure with facts"10-year warranty. First tuning included, three to four weeks after delivery.""Total peace of mind, guaranteed."
5 · Real people, real reviewsA Google review, quoted word for word, with the reviewer's name as it appears there.Invented testimonials, or reviews rewritten to sound better. Australian Consumer Law treats both as misleading.
6 · One next step"Book a visit." One primary action per page, repeated at the end.Three competing buttons in the hero.
Words we use

pre-loved · hand-picked · imported direct · checked in our workshop · showroom · play it · book a visit · first tuning included · white-glove delivery

Words we avoid

used · second-hand · cheap · bargain · best · world-class · unbeatable · stunning · amazing · exclamation marks · "Buy now"
