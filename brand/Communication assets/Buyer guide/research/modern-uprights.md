# Yamaha pre-loved uprights, 2000 onwards: research notes

Web research, 19 September 2026, for the Signature Pianos Yamaha buyer's guide. Primary sources are Yamaha
Japan's own documents: the discontinued-model list with production months [1], the serial-by-year chart [2],
spec pages [3–6] and the silent-unit compatibility list [7]. Dimensions and features of discontinued models
come from Japanese dealer listings, marked (dealer). UNVERIFIED means no source confirmed it.

Serial ranges: Yamaha publishes the first serial of each year for Japan-made pianos [2]; ranges below are
derived from it (≈) and cross-checked against dealer listings with known years. Yamaha notes year boundaries
can shift slightly.

## 1. U1 / U3, 2000s–2010s (export models)

Yamaha Japan lists no U1 or U3 for the Japanese market after the U100 and U300 (both Mar 1994–Aug 1997) [1].
From 1997 Japan used YU names; "U1"/"U3" of the 2000s–2010s are the export versions, built in Japan.

YU33 vs YU30 vs U3: YU33 and U3 are the same size and weight; AU U3 has the same pedals (UK U3 has sostenuto).
YU30 (2001–06) is an earlier design, 152 cm wide, 242 kg, with a tone escape, placed by dealers at YUS3 grade.

YU10 vs YU11: YU10 has a tone escape and ranks higher; YU11 is the plainer successor (228 vs 234 kg, ¥570k vs ¥590k).

| YUS5 (+Wn, MhC) | 2006.09–current | same | 1310×1520×650 | 253 | damper, sostenuto, soft | Ivorite white keys, natural-wood ebony-tone black keys; tone escape in upper and lower front panels (music desk opens forward); no lid prop; walnut hammer mouldings (US); list ¥1,030,000, ¥1,793,000 | 1,5,14 |

YUS1/YUS3 (Yamaha): hammer felt and strings "equivalent to the CFX" in current literature; voiced by skilled
technicians; tone escape at the lower front panel and behind the fallboard; one-position lid prop; fallboard
lock; soft-landing fallboard [5,6,15]. UNVERIFIED: which materials applied before the CFX (2010); dealers cite
CFIIIS-equivalent felt and SU7-equivalent wire on earlier units [34]. YUS5 agraffes: sources disagree
(Piano Buyer yes [17]; a Japanese dealer says dropped on current models [32]).

In 2006 the YUS took over the YU10/30/50 grade and YU11/YU33 became the standard line [29,32]. Variants:
YUS1DE/YUS3DE (2008 special limited), YUS1ASG/YUS3ASG (2007); meanings UNVERIFIED [7,34].

Naming trap: an unrelated older "YUS" was built 1980.09–1982.12 (UX series, serials ≈3.1M–3.68M) [1].

## U1Q / U3Q

The names exist only as distributor/retail stock codes for the current Japan-built U1/U3 (Australia
"U1PEQ"/"U3PEQ", Germany "U1 Q PE", UK "U1Q") [23–28]; Yamaha's own pages call them U1 and U3 [11–13]. In
Australia Q marks the Japan-made version; the Indonesian one is U1JPE [23–26]. What Q stands for, when it
started, and whether it appears on the frame: UNVERIFIED. Yamaha sold no U1/U3 in Japan after August 1997, so a
2000s–2010s piano sourced in Japan carries a YU name (YU1/YU3, YU10/YU30, YU11/YU33), never U1Q/U3Q.
No "U1A/U3A 2008+" exists (U1A/U3A were 1982–87). No 2000s U1H (U1H was 1972–80).

## General notes

- Japan: YU1/3/5 → YU10/30/50 → YU11/33; export U1/U3 for the same era; YUS sold in both. All YU/YUS made at
  Kakegawa; b series Indonesian [31].
- Silent Ensemble recall: YU10/30/50SEB, YUS1/3/5SEB, YM11SXGZ and wood/colour versions, serials
  6,095,681–6,264,935, sold Oct 2004–Sep 2009, about 2,300 units; risk of smoke or fire from the soft-pedal
  control part; free parts replacement [10].
- Japanese mains are 100 V; check a silent unit's adaptor before use in Australia (UNVERIFIED per unit).
- Yamaha Australia's warranty excludes instruments purchased or imported from overseas [16]. Yamaha warns
  against Japan-market pianos; Piano Buyer found the warnings exaggerated but advises humidity control and a
  seller warranty of at least five years [17,18].
- Serial and model are stamped on the upper right of the iron frame. Start-of-year serials (Japan-made) [2]:
  1997 5,520,000 · 1998 5,590,000 · 1999 5,810,000 · 2000 5,860,000 · 2001 5,920,000 · 2002 5,970,000 ·
  2003 6,020,000 · 2004 6,060,000 · 2005 6,100,000 · 2006 6,150,000 · 2007 6,180,000 · 2008 6,220,000 ·
  2009 6,250,000 · 2010 6,280,000 · 2011 6,310,000 · 2012 6,340,000 · 2013 6,360,000 · 2014 6,380,000 ·
  2015 6,400,000 · 2016 6,420,000 · 2017 6,440,000 · 2018 6,460,000 · 2019 6,480,000 · 2020 6,500,000 ·
  2022 6,540,000 · 2025 6,600,000.
- On the YU11 and YUS1, thick music books can be hard to rest on the music desk [3,5].
